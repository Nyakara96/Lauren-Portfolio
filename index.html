About

Boundaries first, then the map.

I'm a land surveyor and GIS specialist based in Kenya, with over five years across cadastral surveying, land administration, and geospatial analysis. My work spans field surveys, boundary and subdivision work, sectional property surveys, and site suitability analysis — supporting land transactions, urban planning, and development projects with data that holds up to scrutiny.

I'm also a contributor to Humanitarian OpenStreetMap, mapping underserved areas in my own time.
